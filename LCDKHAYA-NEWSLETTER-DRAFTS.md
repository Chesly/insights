email tool you send from (Mailchimp, Make.com, etc.), either weekly or grouped into themed
editions. Replace `[First Name]` with your merge-tag syntax and `[Read: ...]` with the real
link to the matching post once it's live at `/lcdkhaya/blog/<slug>`.
